## Bad Apple!! attribution

The players in this repository render the fan-made shadow-art music video commonly known as "Bad Apple!!":

- **Original song**: "Bad Apple!!" composed by ZUN (Team Shanghai Alice), from the Touhou Project game *Lotus Land Story* (東方幻想郷).
- **Arrangement**: "Bad Apple!! feat. nomico" by Masayoshi Minoshima (Alstroemeria Records), vocals by nomico.
- **Shadow-art video**: created by あにら (Anira), originally published on niconico as [sm8628149](https://www.nicovideo.jp/watch/sm8628149) (2009).

## How this repository handles the content

- The video, audio, and frame data used by the OpenTUI player are **not** committed to or distributed with this repository.
- On first run (or via `bun run generate`) the player downloads the video from a public Internet Archive mirror onto **your own machine** and converts it locally for personal playback.
